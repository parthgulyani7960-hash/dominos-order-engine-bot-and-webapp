💡 FAQ & Pricing Rules

❓ How does the Pizza Pricing Cap work?

For orders with subtotals between ₹180.00 and ₹220.00, the base price is capped at a fixed rate of ₹100.00 + the Bot Service Fee. An eligible coupon code is auto-applied!

❓ What if my cart is outside the cap range?

If the subtotal is below ₹180 or above ₹220, the order does not receive a price cap. Instead, a flat ₹5.00 service fee is charged, and no auto-coupon is applied.

❓ What is my benefit from this offer?

You get a pizza order worth up to ₹199 - ₹200 for exactly ₹100! The discount is applied automatically behind the scenes.

❓ How do I order and place it?

Simply add pizzas to your cart. Once your cart subtotal is in the capping range, check out by providing your delivery address, phone number, and location on the map. Pay using your wallet or UPI QR code, and submit the 12-digit UTR to place the order.

❓ How can I pay?

You can pay using your Wallet balance or via direct UPI payment by scanning the QR code and verifying the 12-digit transaction UTR number.
